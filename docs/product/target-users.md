# Target Users

## Individuals

- Families coordinating shared lists and home operations
- Elderly users tracking medicine, appointments, supplies
- Home inventory management
- Medicine tracking
- Food storage tracking

**UX implications:** larger text option, high contrast, simple flows, minimal steps.

## Small businesses

- Parking centers (vehicle entry/exit)
- Local stores (stock, sales support)
- Warehouses (items, locations)
- Workshops (jobs, parts)
- Farmers (livestock, crops, equipment)
- Service centers (appointments, customers)

**UX implications:** fast repetitive entry, barcode/camera later, role-based access.

## Teams

- Operational coordination
- Task workflows
- Shared tracking systems

**UX implications:** permissions, activity history, notifications (later phases).

## Anti-personas (not primary)

- Enterprise IT departments needing SAP-style ERP
- Developers wanting a low-code database builder
- Power users expecting SQL or custom scripting in MVP
